# 考研英语二高分专属模板与论据速查表

## 一、 大作文图表模板速查

### 1. 动态趋势类（折线/跨年柱状）
```text
[首段 - 引入与描述]
Portrayed in the column/line chart is an explicit overview of [主题] in [群体/地点] from [年份] to [年份]. Specifically, [主体A] experienced a steep upward trajectory, surging substantially from [数据1] to a pinnacle of [数据2] in [年份]. In stark contrast, [主体B] witnessed a moderate downward trend, diminishing from [数据3] to [数据4], before leveling out at around [数据5].

[次段 - 双核因果]
Far from being coincidental, the underlying impetus behind such a remarkable shift can be reasonably attributed to multiple intertwined factors. For one thing, [宏观驱动：如 China's sustained economic growth and the continuous rise in residents' disposable income have laid a robust foundation for this trend. Modern citizens enjoy greater financial leeway to allocate funds toward quality-of-life improvements]. Simultaneously, [微观心理/需求：confronted with mounting pressures in daily routines, an increasing number of individuals regard this as an indispensable channel to alleviate mental fatigue and seek personal fulfillment].

[末段 - 前景与建议]
In conclusion, I am firmly convinced that the trend highlighted in the chart will maintain its momentum in the years ahead. Accordingly, it is highly advisable for relevant authorities to optimize current policies and foster a more conducive environment, thereby steering this momentum toward sustainable prosperity.
```

### 2. 静态占比类（饼图/单期柱状）
```text
[首段 - 引入与描述]
Highlighting recent distributions in [主题], the provided pie chart presents an illuminating comparison across distinct categories in [年份]. Specifically, [第一大项] dominates the ranking with a commanding [数据]%, followed closely by [第二项] and [第三项], capturing [数据]% and [数据]% respectively. At the other end of the spectrum, [末尾项] accounts for a negligible [数据]%, representing the smallest proportion.

[次段 - 双核因果]
Far from being coincidental, the underlying impetus behind such disparities can be reasonably attributed to multiple intertwined factors. On the one hand, [技术/社会驱动：the unprecedented advancement of digital platforms and mobile terminals has fundamentally reshaped public lifestyles, offering unparalleled convenience and efficiency]. On the other hand, [观念/认知演变：there has been a paradigm shift in mindsets, with contemporary individuals prioritizing long-term value and personal development over transient gains].

[末段 - 辩证引导与平衡]
To encapsulate, while this phenomenon brings undeniable conveniences, its potential drawbacks warrant sober reflection. It is of vital importance for the youth to strike a healthy balance between [A] and [B]. With rational attitudes and concerted societal guidance, we can ensure that this trend unfolds in a wholesome direction.
```

## 二、 小作文高分模板速查

### 1. 互动回信（Reply to Email / Letter）
```text
Dear [Name/Professor],
    I hope this email finds you well. I was truly delighted to receive your message regarding [核心主题]. I am writing this reply with great enthusiasm to address your inquiries and share some practical suggestions.
    Regarding your concern about [要点一], it is highly recommended that you [动作/措施], which will undoubtedly help you [效果/好处]. Furthermore, with respect to [要点二], [具体安排或细节]. In addition, I would be more than willing to provide any further assistance during the process.
    I sincerely hope the information above proves beneficial to you. Should you need any further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact me. Looking forward to our fruitful collaboration.
                                                            Best regards,
                                                            Li Ming
```
